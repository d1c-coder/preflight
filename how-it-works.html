From unsigned to understood.

The full path a transaction takes through Geno before it goes back to your wallet for signing.

1

Your wallet or dApp starts a transaction

The frontend captures the unsigned parameters: chain ID, sender, destination, value, calldata and gas.

2

Sent to the Preview API

The request is validated, and contract and token metadata are resolved.

3

Decoded

The target contract and function are identified, and arguments are decoded where an ABI is available.

4

Simulated

The transaction executes against a read-only fork or simulation RPC. Nothing is broadcast.

5

State-diffed and risk-checked

Pre and post state are compared for asset and permission changes, then run through deterministic warning rules.

6

Returned to you, unchanged

You see the normalized preview. The original transaction goes back to your wallet exactly as it was. Geno never alters it.
